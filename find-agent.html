Why instruct an agent to sell your property?

A good agent earns their fee. Alongside marketing your home to the right buyers, an experienced local agent will:

  • Recommend an asking price based on real, up-to-date local knowledge.
  • Use their understanding of the local market to reach the right buyers.
  • Present your home at its best to get genuine interest and viewings.
  • Give you honest feedback and reporting on how your listing is performing.
  • Handle negotiations and the often tricky route to completion on your behalf.

Benefits of using a letting agent

If you\u2019re letting a property, a good agent can take a lot off your plate:

  • Reduce the stress and admin of finding and referencing tenants.
  • Offer guidance on compliance, safety certificates and your responsibilities.
  • Manage the property day to day \u2014 especially helpful if you live far away.
  • Market your rental correctly so the right tenants apply quickly.

How to choose an estate agent

Once you\u2019ve decided to sell or let, compare a shortlist before you instruct. It helps to look at recent sales or lets in your area, the fees and contract terms, and how each agent plans to market your property.

Ask for examples of similar homes they\u2019ve handled, how they\u2019ll price yours, and how they communicate. The cheapest fee isn\u2019t always the best value \u2014 a great agent can more than pay for themselves.

Talk to your agent about standing out

Small touches help your property stand out from others on the market. Ask your agent about a Premium or Featured listing, a Property Passport and great photography to make a strong first impression.
